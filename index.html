Controller & settings

Learn: hover a knob, click L, then move a controller knob or fader. ⚙ sets its range. Mappings follow the parameter (e.g. “A volume”), whatever voice is selected. They're kept in this browser.

The SY22 can't take single-parameter changes, so every edit sends the whole voice (592 bytes, about 0.2 s of MIDI) to its edit buffer. Stay in VOICE play mode on the SY22, not EDIT, while editing live.
